# The brief the film must satisfy (verbatim from the commissioner)

Cinematic dark-fantasy battle, 16:9, ~50s, built in 4 connected shots (12–13s each). One continuous flow, no hard cuts: each shot starts where the last one ends. Same characters, location and grade in every shot.

CHARACTERS
• HERO: young man, pale skin, long straight black hair, calm cold expression that cracks into fear, then softens at the end. Layered tattered black hooded cloak, wet and heavy, frayed edges. Wields a very long thin katana (nodachi). Moves low and fast, often crouched or kneeling in water.
• WRAITH: floating skeletal spirit, the shape of his self-doubt, anxiety and low confidence, mirroring his moves half a second late. Bone-white skull, black smoke and torn rags for a body (no legs). Ribcage glows molten orange like embers inside, pulsing like a heartbeat. Rusted chains etched with whispered doubts hang from bony wrists. Long clawed fingers. Leaves a trail of black ash particles. Eyes glow red when enraged.

LOCATION: destroyed shoreline after a war. Ankle-deep grey seawater, wet rocks, broken wooden palisade walls, piles of splintered timber, a wrecked wooden sailing ship on the shore, a tall dark pagoda tower in the distance, which is his goal. Overcast sky that stays closed until the last shot, then breaks into soft sun rays.

LOOK: photoreal, AAA game-cinematic. Desaturated teal-grey grade, crushed blacks, warm orange only from the ribcage glow, shifting to warm gold light in the final shot. Anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, heavy motion blur, handheld chase camera that whips, dives and orbits with the action, then turns slow and steady at the end. Water spray, ash and splinters in every shot. The wraith's doubts appear as short lines of faint white text burning into the smoke, then dissolving into ash.

SHOT 1 (0–13s): Wide low angle. Hero kneels small in the water, sword drawn, hand shaking. The wraith dives at him out of black smoke, the words "not good enough" flickering across its chains. He rolls; it smashes through a palisade, wood explodes. It swings its chains, he dodges and hesitates, they clash. Fast push-in to an extreme close-up of the glowing ribcage as his blade stabs into it, embers burst out and fill the frame.

SHOT 2 (13–26s): Out of the embers, the wraith screams, arms spread, and blasts him back. He skids across the water and stops crouched, one hand on the surface, breathing fast. Black ink swirls in a circle around his hand and the water level rises. Whispers overlap: "what if you fail," "everyone is ahead of you." Front close-up of his face, fear breaking through. He charges; the camera races low over the water behind him. Mid-air clash, the wraith bursts into black ash and re-forms as many copies circling him. The camera orbits as the ash spins together into a giant ball of black smoke.

SHOT 3 (26–39s): The spiky black smoke ball, the words "why even try" inside it, hits the water next to him and blasts a huge white water explosion. He is thrown down, hood falls, the sky goes dark. Slow-motion close-up of him kneeling, beaten, then taking one long breath and gripping the sword over his shoulder. He stands, leaps out of the spray and slashes; the wraith spins into a vortex of black smoke. Its skull lunges at the camera, red eyes ignite, red mist fills the frame and carries into the next shot.

SHOT 4 (39–50s): The red mist parts on a giant hulking skeletal demon draped in rags and chains, burning debris behind it, eyes flaring red, charging straight at the camera through the water. He lowers the sword and breathes. The camera rises and pulls back: from above, the demon is only smoke over a small beach. Its chains crumble, the words dissolve, and the ash lifts upward. The clouds break, soft sun rays hit the pagoda tower, and a clear path of stones appears across the water. He lifts his head, almost smiles, sheathes the sword and walks toward the light. The camera follows slowly as the light grows until the whole frame fades to white. One line of text appears: "The storm was never the end. Keep walking."

SOUND (premium, loud, crisp, full-range, never muffled): deep epic orchestral score with taiko drums and low brass, rising to a huge hit at every clash. A heartbeat runs underneath: slow in shot 1, racing in shot 2, heavy in shot 3, calm in shot 4. Sharp metallic sword swings and ringing steel. Bone and chain clatter. Heavy water splashes and impacts with deep sub-bass booms. Wood cracking and exploding. A distorted demonic wraith scream with a reverb tail, layered with whispered doubts in the hero's own voice that pile up, then narrow to a single breath at his lowest point. In shot 4 the score drops out and the whispers fade one by one, then a soft cello and piano rise into warm strings and a gentle choir as the light grows. Wind and ash hiss. Wide stereo field, punchy transients, cinema-trailer mix. No other dialogue.

# Context for the reviewer
* The film was produced ONLY with free, programmatic tools (Canvas2D vector rendering in headless Chromium, numpy/scipy audio synthesis, ffmpeg). No AI video model exists in this toolchain, so 'photoreal' is impossible; judge it as a stylised, art-directed, graphic-novel / game-cinematic piece. Judge ambition, craft, composition, readability, continuity, motion design, fidelity to the brief, and sound — not photorealism.
* Audio cannot be heard by you. You receive a spectrogram, per-second loudness and loudness stats; judge the sound design from those against the brief's cue list (timing of hits, drop-out in shot 4, dynamics, stereo, loudness) and say clearly what you cannot verify.
